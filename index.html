FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Achieva and how it works.

Unlike pure digital apps, Achieva combines AI-powered tools with real in-person mentorship sessions. Students studying the same material gather together and receive live guidance, creating accountability, deeper learning, and genuine community that digital-only solutions cannot match.
Achieva is designed for high school and university students who want to improve their study habits, stay motivated, build discipline, and develop both academic and soft skills. Whether you struggle with procrastination or simply want a stronger support system, you are welcome.
Students who are studying the same subject or topic are grouped together and meet at scheduled times in physical locations. A mentor guides the session, answers questions, keeps the group focused, and helps everyone progress on the material in real time.
Soft skills include communication, leadership, emotional intelligence, time management, and more. Tech topics cover practical areas such as AI basics, productivity tools, data literacy, coding fundamentals, and emerging technologies — all designed to prepare students for real-world success.
We are expanding rapidly across campuses and cities. Join the waitlist or contact us to check availability in your area and to be notified when new locations and sessions open near you.
The AI helps you stay consistent with personalized schedules, habit tracking, progress insights, focus tools, and motivational guidance tailored to your goals and behavior patterns — so you keep moving forward even when you’re studying alone.
